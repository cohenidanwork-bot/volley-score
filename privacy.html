Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Volley Score is a volleyball scoreboard. It works offline, needs no account and asks for no permissions on your phone. This policy explains the little data involved and why.

What stays on your phone

Team names, colours, scores and your match history are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Subscriptions

Volley Score Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google take the payment; we never see your card or bank details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random user ID created by the app on your device (not your name, email or Apple / Google account);
  • the purchase history of this app: product, price, currency, dates and the store's transaction ID;
  • technical data needed to answer the request: app version, operating system, the country of your store account and your IP address.

This is used only to unlock the app, restore your purchase on a new phone and count sales. The legal basis is the performance of the contract you entered into when you subscribed. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

How long

Purchase records are kept for as long as you have a subscription and afterwards as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data linked to your purchase, or object to its use. Because the user ID is random, include your order number (Apple: from the receipt email; Google: starts with GPA.) so we can find it. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

The app has no objectionable content, but it is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Informativa sulla privacy
