Keep score like the officials

Volleyball scoreboard for iPhone and Android · Marcador de voleibol · Segnapunti pallavolo

Tap a team’s side of the court to give them the point. Volley Score follows the FIVB rules for indoor and beach: sets to 25 or 21, tie-break to 15, two-point lead, side changes, time-outs and who serves. Every finished match is kept in your history.

Support

Restore my subscription on a new phone
Open the app, go to the subscription screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
Cancel my subscription
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Volley Score. Android: Play Store → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Volley Score. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
My match history didn’t move to my new phone
History is stored only on the phone where the match was played. Nothing is uploaded.
The wrong team is serving
Open the menu in the net strip and tap Change server, or undo the last point.
